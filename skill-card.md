## Description:

Provides Chinese-language CGMA / GMAP management accounting, strategic finance, performance management, valuation, investment analysis, global tax compliance, AI governance, and leadership guidance — upgraded with the **Rise2040 (R!SE 2040) foresight framework** from AICPA & CIMA.

Version 3.0.0 adds a structured Hard Trend / Soft Trend methodology, the five drivers of change and their opportunity matrices, the 2040 four-category / 18-capability model (mapped to the CGMA 2019 five domains), an eight-seat expert deliberation panel with a binding adjudication hierarchy, six end-to-end engagement playbooks, fill-in templates and checklists, explicit governance boundaries, and an authoritative EN–ZH glossary. The main SKILL.md is a progressive-disclosure controller that routes to seven reference modules.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

| Risk | Mitigation |
|------|------------|
| Broad finance triggers could produce investment, management-accounting, or jurisdictionally ambiguous guidance | Specify market, jurisdiction, and whether the response is general education rather than personalized advice; every deliverable carries a disclaimer and an escalation path |
| Foresight content (Hard Trends / Soft Trends, 2040 vision) could be misread as a prediction or a mandate | Soft Trends are always labelled `[假设]`; the skill states that the report is an invitation, not a directive; no single future is asserted |
| Multi-seat expert framing could be mistaken for institutional endorsement | Explicit statement that the panel is a set of methodological perspectives and does not represent any organisation's official position |
| Valuation, tax, legal, and assurance outputs could be relied on as professional opinions | Outputs are framed as frameworks, ranges, and review checklists; licensed-professional review is mandated for legal, tax, assurance, and filing conclusions |
| Copyrighted source material could be reproduced without permission | Only summarised, paraphrased, and methodologised content is included; citation format and the AICPA/CIMA permissions contact are provided; no verbatim full-text reproduction |
| AI-generated content could bypass human validation | A mandatory human-in-the-loop verification gate, model cards, data lineage, logging, and deactivation thresholds are specified |
| Regional regulations and AI rules change after the knowledge cut-off | Jurisdiction + effective-date verification is mandatory; users must not treat stored knowledge as current law |
